About Lok Setuvi AI

Public services can be confusing when information is difficult to find or understand. Lok Setuvi AI aims to make that information more accessible through simple, clear guidance.

Our goal is to develop a helpful tool that directs people to official sources and explains processes in easy language.

Our Mission

📚 Understand

Explain public-service information in clear, simple language.

🔎 Verify

Help users find official sources instead of relying on guesses.

🤝 Include

Explore guidance that can serve people with different levels of digital experience.

Our Current Stage

We are developing the project concept and exploring a prototype. The AI assistant is not yet presented as a fully operational public service.
